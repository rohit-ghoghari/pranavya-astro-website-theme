## 3. Items Requiring Buyer Verification or Replacement

The assets below are bundled **strictly for preview and demonstration purposes**. No transferable commercial resale or sublicensing rights are conveyed for these preview assets. Theme buyers must audit, license, or replace them before launching a production commercial website.

### 3.1 Audio Tracks (`public/audio/`)
- **Files:**
  - `public/audio/health.mp3`
  - `public/audio/health-2.mp3`
- **Status:** Demonstration audio tracks.
- **Buyer Requirement:** These files do not carry embedded royalty-free commercial resale licenses. Buyers must replace them with properly licensed commercial audio, royalty-free meditation music, or original studio recordings before launching.

### 3.2 Video Footage (`public/video/`)
- **Files:**
  - `public/video/podcast-video-1.mp4` through `public/video/podcast-video-8.mp4`
  - `public/video/product-detail-1.mp4`
  - `public/video/yoga-class.mp4`
  - `public/video/yoga.mp4`
- **Status:** Stock video clips used for demo video player and hero banner demonstrations.
- **Buyer Requirement:** Buyers must replace these video files with their own recorded footage or appropriately licensed stock clips (e.g., from Envato Elements, Adobe Stock, Pexels, or Shutterstock) before commercial distribution.

### 3.3 Stock Imagery & Photographs (`public/*.webp`, `src/content/**/*.md`)
- **Files:** All yoga studio, instructor portrait, retreat location, and product showcase photography.
- **Status:** Royalty-free demonstration imagery sourced from stock photography repositories.
- **Buyer Requirement:** Images are included to demonstrate theme layout and visual structure. Resale marketplaces do not allow repackaging third-party stock images as standalone downloadable assets. Buyers must substitute these images with client-specific photography or properly licensed commercial stock photos.

### 3.4 Third-Party Trademarks & Platform Logos
- **Files:**
  - Vector platform icons: `public/spotify.svg`, `public/itunes.svg` (Apple Podcasts), `public/stitcher.svg`
  - Payment brand icons: `public/visa.webp`, `public/mastercard.webp`, `public/paypal.webp`
- **Status:** Registered trademarks and service marks owned by their respective rights holders:
  - **Spotify** is a trademark of Spotify AB.
  - **Apple Podcasts / iTunes** is a trademark of Apple Inc.
  - **Stitcher** is a trademark of Stitcher Media LLC / SiriusXM.
  - **Visa** is a registered trademark of Visa International Service Association.
  - **Mastercard** is a registered trademark of Mastercard International Incorporated.
  - **PayPal** is a registered trademark of PayPal, Inc.
- **Buyer Requirement:** Included strictly for demonstration of platform badges and payment footer icons. Use must conform to each company’s brand identity guidelines and trademark policies, or be replaced with generic vector icons.
